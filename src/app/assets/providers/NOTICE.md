# Provider and agent logos

These SVGs identify the model vendors and coding-agent CLIs that Magies Office
can be configured to talk to. They are inlined by `src/app/ai/providerIcons.ts`
so the full-colour ones keep their own colours and the monochrome ones follow
the interface theme.

Using a vendor's mark to identify that vendor in a configuration list is
nominative use; the marks themselves remain the property of their owners, and
their presence here implies no affiliation with, or endorsement by, any of them.
